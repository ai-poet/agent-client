# Artwork provenance

All distributed raster assets were generated with OpenAI imagegen for this package. Version 0.2.0 replaced the hero with an original virtual idol who stands on a night-city banner and rises above it, and regenerated the dark wallpaper, the mode icons, the corner brackets and the button light trace from one asset sheet. The light wallpaper and the card circuit texture are retained from the first release.

The character was drawn on flat green and keyed to transparency; the asset sheet was drawn on black, keyed by brightness and sliced. PNGs are downscaled for their display roles. UI controls, labels and interactions remain Sailry-owned.

## Generation prompts

### background

Use case: background-extraction. Asset: production full-window desktop wallpaper, wide 2048 by 1280 PNG. Reference is the approved theme concept. Extract/recreate ONLY the illustrated environment, remove character and EVERY UI element, sidebar, cards, composer, words and logo completely. Preserve the approved beautiful anime holographic sky city: bright azure/cyan spacious sky with pink-lavender clouds, distant futuristic city skyline across bottom, tallest neon cyan/pink buildings and floating holographic projections along far RIGHT edge. Left edge has distant low towers and pastel clouds; broad central sky open clean. Holographic projection panes show subtle abstract data marks but absolutely no readable writing, symbols, logos or paw motifs. A few small floating translucent facets along peripheral corners. Mostly cyan and soft blue, pink as accent. Same detailed anime painterly style, cinematic depth and luminous atmosphere as reference. NOT a stage, no platform or crowd, no giant ribbons, no petals, no flowers, no character. Fill entire image with environment, no frame. Real production background art only, no transparent cutouts, no UI or text.

### circuit

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: seamlessly repeating transparent PNG microtexture for desktop app cards, 512 by 512 square. Sparse fine rose-pink holographic circuit traces and tiny lavender square nodes, evenly distributed at very low visual density over real transparent background. Traces simple crisp thin lines, mostly straight with 45-degree junctions and occasional small node rings, no crowded PCB pattern. Truly seamless repeat both directions, all marks stop well inside outer 20 pixels so borders fully transparent. Color pink with restrained cyan secondary microdots, no glow clouds, no opaque base, no white canvas, no labels, no words, no frame, no icons, no gradients in background. Quiet subtle technological surface material, not star confetti, not pixels.

### circuit edge correction

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit this transparent pink circuit texture for seamless tiling. Preserve its sparse small pink technological traces and a few cyan microdots, but REMOVE ALL marks anywhere in the outermost 12 percent margin on EVERY side. The full border band must be genuinely transparent and entirely empty. Move remaining marks inward so NONE touch or cross the image edges. No connecting lines at seams. Central 76 percent contains only sparse thin simple pink circuit strokes evenly scattered, keep 90 percent of entire canvas transparent. Tile should be extremely quiet at 160px. Actual alpha transparency, no background colors, no glow haze, no text, no checkerboard. Entire continuous empty border guarantees seamless repeats.

### figure

Shoulder-length wavy silver-lavender hair fading to cyan and pink at the tips, a small star hair clip, a slim crystal ear-piece. - A modest, fully covering idol stage outfit: a fitted white-and-lavender stage jacket with high collar, worn closed and on both shoulders, holographic trim and small star buttons, short cape detail, gloves. No bare shoulders, no cleavage, no choker, no straps, nothing revealing. - Expression and pose of a bright, energetic, innocent idol: a friendly closed-mouth or small open smile, clear sparkling eyes looking at the viewer, a confident cheerful wave with one gloved hand raised beside her face (palm open, waving), the other hand out of frame. Nothing in her hands. Not flirtatious, not blushing heavily, no seductive look. - Waist-up, three-quarter view, body turned slightly left, filling the frame vertically; the bottom edge cuts cleanly at the waist. - Portrait canvas 3:4. A very wide 4:1 full-bleed banner scene (e.g. 2048x512), NO characters or people, NO frame, NO rounded corners, NO text or logos: - Dreamy holographic riverside night city: indigo and violet skyscrapers with abstract equalizer-bar holograms, a large luminous moon with a thin holographic ring at the left third, a concert stadium with soft light beams at the right, aurora ribbons in cyan and rose pink, city lights reflected on calm water at the bottom, a few drifting light petals. - The right third (a character will stand in front) has no focal object at its center; the far left is a little calmer.
